Overview

GTBIT’s first virtual physics lab brings five experiments across mechanics, optics and circuits into the browser.

The lab supports practice at home, saved observations and exported readings for lab reports. I co-built it with three teammates under faculty mentorship.

Workflow

  1. 01

    Choose an experiment

    Explore experiments across mechanics, optics and circuits.

  2. 02

    Practise

    Interact with the experiment and observe its behaviour from home.

  3. 03

    Record observations

    Save observations and export readings for a lab report.

Engineering

My contribution

I developed the RC circuit experiment. It lets students observe capacitor charging and determine the circuit’s time constant.

Team delivery

The wider virtual lab was built by a four-member team with faculty mentorship. The site uses Next.js and is deployed on Vercel.

Limitations

The simulations support practice and observation. This case study does not claim measured learning gains or validation against physical laboratory equipment.
